Executive Mission Summary

The SDMCET Airborne quadcopter successfully completed all autonomous waypoints with zero geofence violations. The custom claw payload mechanism actuated flawlessly at Target Alpha, releasing the 1.2kg payload within a 0.5m CEP (Circular Error Probable). The carbon fiber chassis demonstrated excellent structural integrity under payload release stress. Telemetry and video links remained >95% stable.
